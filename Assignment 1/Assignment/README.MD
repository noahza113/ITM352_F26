I stored the questions and hints, organized by category, in a JSON file that the quiz imports. This keeps the questions, hints, and categories separate from the quiz logic, making them easier to edit and reuse in other quizzes.

At the end of the quiz, players with at least one point can choose to risk their points in a double-or-nothing round. A correct answer doubles their score, while an incorrect answer reduces it to zero. This adds an engaging challenge and gives students a chance to earn back points they may need.

If a user enters an invalid choice, the quiz explains the valid options and prompts them again. This helps prevent errors and keeps the quiz simple to use.

I used AI to help me implement the features I wanted. I also used it to generate the questions and hints, make most of the requested changes, answer questions, spell and grammar check, and help fix bugs.
